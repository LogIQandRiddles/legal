Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Who we are

LogIQ & Riddles Publisher is an internal publishing tool built and operated by the LogIQ & Riddles project (a sole proprietorship registered in Poland, operating under the e-magination.pl business portfolio). It is used solely to publish puzzle content — images, video clips, and captions — to the LogIQ & Riddles Facebook Page and Instagram account.

What this app is — and isn't

This app is not a public product and does not have external end users. It is a single-purpose automation tool used by its own developer to schedule and publish content to accounts they own and administer. It does not offer a login, sign-up, or any feature for members of the public to connect their own Facebook or Instagram accounts.

Information we access

Through the Meta Graph API, the app accesses only what is necessary to publish and manage content on the connected Page and Instagram account, specifically:

  • Page and Instagram Business account identifiers needed to address API requests.
  • Access tokens issued by Meta that authorize the app to publish posts, reels, and captions, and to manage comments and messages related to that published content.
  • Basic delivery status information returned by the API (for example, whether a post was published successfully) so the app can record it in its own internal dashboard.

The app does not collect, request, or store personal information belonging to followers, commenters, or message senders. It does not read, export, or process end-user profile data beyond what Meta's API returns as part of standard content publishing and comment/message management.

How we use this information

Access tokens and account identifiers are used exclusively to publish scheduled puzzle content to the connected Page and Instagram account, and to let the developer manage the resulting comments and messages from a single dashboard. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to operating this publishing tool.

How we store and protect it

Access tokens are stored on infrastructure controlled by the developer, are not transmitted to or shared with any third party, and are never sold. Access to the systems that hold these tokens is limited to the developer.

Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share any information obtained through the Meta Platforms with any third party, for any purpose.

Data retention

Access tokens are retained only for as long as the app is actively connected to the Page and Instagram account. Removing the app's access (see Data Deletion Instructions) causes any stored tokens to stop working immediately and they are deleted from our systems.

Children's privacy

This app is an internal business tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Google Calendar

LogIQ & Riddles Publisher also connects to a single Google Calendar ("LogIQ & Riddles — Publikacje"), owned by the same operator, to keep the publishing schedule synchronized. The tool creates, updates, and reads calendar events reflecting scheduled and published content. No other Google Calendar data is accessed, and no data is shared with, sold to, or processed on behalf of any third party. Access can be revoked at any time via Google Account permissions.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: August 2026

Because LogIQ & Riddles Publisher is an internal tool that only operates on accounts owned and administered by the LogIQ & Riddles project, it does not hold personal data belonging to third parties. The steps below explain how the account administrator can revoke the app's access and have any retained data deleted.

Removing the app's access immediately stops all API calls and deletes the associated access token from our systems.

Step 1 — Remove access via Facebook

  1. Open Facebook Settings & Privacy → Settings → Business Integrations (or, on a Business Page, Meta Business Suite → Settings → Connected Apps).
  2. Find LogIQ & Riddles Publisher in the list of connected apps.
  3. Select Remove to revoke its access to the Page.

Step 2 — Remove access via Instagram

  1. Open the Instagram app or instagram.com/accounts/manage_access.
  2. Find LogIQ & Riddles Publisher under connected apps.
  3. Select Remove Access.

Step 3 — Request confirmation (optional)

To request written confirmation that any retained data has been deleted, email logiqandriddles@gmail.com with the subject line "Data deletion request." We will confirm deletion within 30 days.

Because this app does not collect data from Facebook or Instagram users other than the LogIQ & Riddles account administrator, there is no separate end-user data to delete beyond the access token itself.
